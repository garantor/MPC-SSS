# MPC-Prive Wallet: The Future of Secure Web3 Identity

Welcome to **MPC-Prive**, a next-generation crypto wallet designed to make self-custody secure, simple, and accessible for everyone. We combine advanced cryptography with the ease of use you expect from modern apps.

## 🌟 Why MPC-Prive?

Traditional crypto wallets require you to manage complex "seed phrases" (lists of 12-24 words). If you lose them, you lose your money. If someone steals them, they steal your money.

**MPC-Prive solves this.**

We use **Multi-Party Computation (MPC)** and **Shamir's Secret Sharing** to split your private key into invisible "shares". These shares are never stored in one place, making your wallet significantly harder to hack and impossible to lose casually.

### ✨ Key Features

*   **🚫 No More Seed Phrases**: You never have to write down or memorize a secret phrase. Your account is secured by your device and your cloud backup.
*   **🔑 Passkey Login**: Access your wallet instantly using **FaceID**, **TouchID**, or your device's PIN. Standard passwords are a thing of the past.
*   **🌍 True Multi-Chain Support**: Manage assets across all major blockchains from a single dashboard:
    *   **Ethereum (EVM)**
    *   **Solana**
    *   **Stellar**
    *   **XRP (Ripple)**
*   **☁️ Secure Cloud Recovery**: One part of your key is encrypted and safely stored in your own **Google Drive** (App Folder). If you lose your phone, you can recover your wallet in seconds on a new device.

## 🔒 How It Works (Simplified)

Imagine your house key is split into two pieces. You need both to open the door, but you only ever have to hold one piece, while the other is securely locked away in a vault you own.

1.  **Your Device (Share 1)**: A piece of your key is stored securely on your phone or computer, protected by your Passkey (FaceID/TouchID).
2.  **The Cloud (Share 2)**: The second piece is encrypted and stored in your personal cloud account.
3.  **The Magic**: When you log in, the app momentarily combines these pieces to sign transactions, then immediately separates them again. Your full key never sits exposed on a server or your device's permanent storage.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1.  **Sign Up**: Create an account using your Passkey (Biometrics).
2.  **Encrypt**: The app automatically splits your key and encrypts the backup share.
3.  **Backup**: Sign in with Google to save your recovery share to your private Drive.
4.  **Transact**: You're ready! Receive or send ETH, SOL, XLM, and XRP instantly.
